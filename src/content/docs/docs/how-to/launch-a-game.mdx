This guide will cover how a bot made with the [SDK](/plugins/com.botmaker.sdk/) starts its game: a Steam game, a
Windows game through Bottles, an Android game in Waydroid or on a phone over `adb`, and on Linux a private display
session so the bot plays without taking over your screen.
